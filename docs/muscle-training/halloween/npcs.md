Pumpkins are given to everyone who hit the NPC in the last 20 seconds (full reward each). NPCs respawn 5 seconds after dying.

| NPC | HP | ATK | Pumpkins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zombie | 50 | 15 | 5 |
| Skeleton | 2.5K | 250 | 65 |
| Ghost | 150K | 5K | 1.95K |
| Witch | 10M | 45K | 8.75K |
| Werewolf | 700M | 120K | 224K |
| Pumpkin Knight | 50B | 850K | 1.5M |
