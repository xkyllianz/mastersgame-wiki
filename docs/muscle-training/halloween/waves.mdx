# Nightmare Waves (infinite mode)

Same HP and damage for everyone. No resources: it’s for records, the leaderboard and titles.

| Brute | Wave 7 | Tanky |
| Nightmare Lord | Every 10 waves | Mini-boss with AoE slam |

Enemies get **+6% HP** and **+5% ATK** per wave.
